# Listening test

You will hear 20 pairs of short speech recordings (A and B), each up to 4 seconds. Play A, then B, as often as you like. Use headphones and keep the volume the same for the whole test.

For every pair, fill in one row of the answer sheet (`answer_sheet_template.csv`, one copy per listener):

- `listener_id`: your initials or a number (the same on every row).
- `hear_difference`: `yes` if A and B sound different in any way, otherwise `no`.
- `style_A`, `style_B`: the speaking style you hear in A and in B, one of: confused, default, enunciated, happy, laughing, sad, whisper; or `unsure`.

There are no right or wrong answers. Do not compare notes with other listeners.
