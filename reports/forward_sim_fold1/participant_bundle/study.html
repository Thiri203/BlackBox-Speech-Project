Guess what the computer model said

A computer model listens to short speech clips and decides which of 7 speaking styles it hears. It is sometimes wrong. Your task: guess the style the model chose (not necessarily the style you hear).

Before you start

You will hear 20 clips in two parts of 10. Use headphones and keep the volume the same throughout. Play each clip as often as you like. There is no feedback, and you cannot go back to change an answer.

Part 2: clips with highlights

Part 1 is done. In part 2 you will hear 10 new clips. Under each clip there is a picture of the sound (time from left to right, pitch from bottom to top, brighter = louder). The clip is cut into 250 ms pieces. Boxes 1, 2 and 3 mark the three pieces that an explanation method says mattered most for the model's answer (1 = most). Grey hatched area = no audio. The task is the same: guess the style the model chose.
